THE SCIENCE OF FLAVOR

Food of the Gods

Our cocoa beans are scientifically known as Theobroma Cacao, which literally translates to "Food of the Gods." Harvested by hand from the nutrient-rich volcanic soils of Mount Apo, we treat every pod with divine reverence. We avoid machines that could damage the delicate trees, ensuring only the most perfect beans make the cut.

The magic happens after the harvest. Our beans undergo a meticulous fermentation process - a crucial phase that unlocks deep, complex chocolate notes. Packed with mood-enhancing compounds like theobromine and rich antioxidants, our chocolate doesn't just taste good; it creates a natural high. This is the "Maxx Overdose" experience.
