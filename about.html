Our Story

Brewed with Heart

At Maxx Overdose Coffee Shop, we know a good cup of coffee can brighten your whole day. That’s why we make every drink with care - brewed fresh, crafted with love, and made to feel like it came from a friend who understands exactly what you need. From the aroma to the first sip, we want each cup to bring comfort, warmth, and a little boost to keep you going.

And because life gets busy, we bring our café experience straight to your home or office. Whether you're starting your morning, pushing through work, or taking a well-deserved break, we deliver your favorite coffee freshly made and full of flavor. No waiting, no hassle - just good coffee, delivered with heart.
